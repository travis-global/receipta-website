Privacy Policy

Last updated: April, 2026.

Receipta respects the privacy of its users and is committed to protecting personal and business information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when users interact with Receipta services.

Scope of Services

Receipta is a transaction logging and record-keeping service designed for businesses operating via messaging platforms such as WhatsApp. The platform does not provide financial, banking, or payment processing services.

Information We Collect

Receipta may collect the following categories of information:

  • Business name and business-related contact details
  • Phone numbers used to interact with the service
  • Transaction data voluntarily submitted by users
  • Messages sent to Receipta through supported platforms such as WhatsApp
  • Basic technical data required to maintain system functionality

How Information Is Collected

Information is collected only when users voluntarily interact with Receipta. This includes sending messages, submitting transaction data, or contacting support. Receipta does not collect information from personal conversations outside of messages intentionally sent to the service.

How Information Is Used

Collected data is used strictly for service-related purposes, including:

  • Processing transaction messages into structured records
  • Maintaining organized financial logs for users
  • Improving system performance and reliability
  • Providing user support and responding to inquiries

WhatsApp and Messaging Platform Usage

Receipta operates through messaging platforms such as WhatsApp. All interactions are initiated by users. Receipta does not send unsolicited messages, marketing messages, or automated outreach to users.

Only messages intentionally sent to Receipta for transaction recording purposes are processed. Receipta does not access or monitor private conversations outside of user-initiated interactions.

User Consent (Opt-In Policy)

By sending messages to Receipta, users provide explicit consent for those messages to be processed and stored for transaction recording purposes. Users may discontinue use of the service at any time by ceasing interaction.

Data Storage and Retention

Receipta stores transaction data and related information only for as long as necessary to provide its services and maintain records. Reasonable steps are taken to ensure data is securely stored and protected against unauthorized access.

Data Sharing

Receipta does not sell, rent, or trade user data. Information may only be shared:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect the rights and safety of the service or its users
  • With service providers strictly for operational purposes (if applicable)

Data Protection

Receipta implements reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect user data from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Third-Party Platforms

Receipta relies on third-party platforms such as WhatsApp for communication. Use of these platforms is subject to their respective privacy policies and terms. Receipta is not responsible for how these third parties handle data.

User Rights

Users have the right to:

  • Request access to their data
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of their data (subject to service limitations)
  • Stop using the service at any time

Limitations of Service

Receipta is not a financial institution and does not store, process, or transfer money. The service only records and structures transaction data submitted by users.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in services or regulatory requirements. Updates will be published on this page.
