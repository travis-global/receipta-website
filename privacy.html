Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Receipta respects the privacy of its users and is committed to protecting personal and business information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when businesses interact with Receipta services.

Information We Collect

Receipta may collect information necessary to operate its services, including:

  • Business name and contact details
  • Phone numbers used to interact with the service
  • Transaction information submitted by businesses
  • Messages sent through supported communication channels such as WhatsApp

How Information Is Used

Information collected through Receipta is used solely for the purpose of operating and improving the service. This includes:

  • Processing transaction messages submitted by businesses
  • Generating structured transaction records and receipts
  • Improving service reliability and functionality
  • Providing customer support when required

Transaction Data

Businesses may submit transaction information such as item details, quantities, and prices. This information is processed to create structured transaction records and digital receipts that help businesses maintain organized financial records.

Data Protection

Receipta takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

Third-Party Platforms

Receipta may operate through third-party communication platforms such as WhatsApp. Use of these platforms may also be subject to their own privacy policies and terms of service.

Data Sharing

Receipta does not sell or rent user data to third parties. Information is only used to operate the service or comply with applicable legal obligations.

User Responsibility

Businesses are responsible for ensuring that the information they submit through Receipta does not violate any laws or third-party rights.

Policy Updates

Receipta may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in services or legal requirements. Updated policies will be published on this page.
