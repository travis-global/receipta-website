Privacy Policy

Last updated: April, 2026 • View Data Deletion Instructions

Receipta respects the privacy of its users and is committed to protecting personal and business information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when users interact with Receipta services.

Scope of Services

Receipta is a transaction logging and record-keeping service designed for businesses operating via messaging platforms such as WhatsApp. The platform does not provide financial, banking, or payment processing services.

Information We Collect

  • Business name and business-related contact details
  • Phone numbers used to interact with the service
  • Transaction data voluntarily submitted by users
  • Messages sent through supported platforms such as WhatsApp
  • Basic technical data required for system functionality

How Information Is Used

  • Processing transaction messages into structured records
  • Maintaining organized financial logs
  • Improving service reliability
  • Providing support

WhatsApp Usage

Receipta only processes messages sent voluntarily by users. No personal chats are accessed, and no unsolicited messages are sent.

User Consent

By interacting with Receipta, users consent to data processing necessary for providing the service.

Data Storage

Data is stored securely and retained only as long as necessary for service operation.

Data Sharing

Receipta does not sell user data. Data may only be shared when required by law.

User Rights

  • Access their data
  • Correct inaccuracies
  • Request deletion
  • Stop using the service anytime

Account & Data Deletion

Receipta allows users to permanently delete their account and all associated data at any time.

To delete your account:

  • Send: DELETE MY ACCOUNT
  • Click YES to confirm
  • Enter your registered name for verification

This ensures only authorized users can delete accounts.

What Happens After

  • Your account is permanently deleted
  • All transaction records are removed
  • A confirmation document is sent to you

Important

  • Deletion is irreversible
  • Data cannot be recovered
  • Some data may be retained if legally required
