How Receipta Uses WhatsApp

Receipta operates as a transaction logging service designed for businesses that run their daily operations through WhatsApp.


Users voluntarily send structured transaction messages to Receipta. The system processes only these submitted messages into organized financial records.


Receipta does not initiate conversations, send unsolicited messages, or access personal chats. All interactions are user-initiated (opt-in) and comply with WhatsApp Business messaging policies.
