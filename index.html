About Receipta Ledgers

Building financial discipline for everyday Nigerian businesses.

Receipta Ledgers exists to help micro, small and informal businesses develop consistent financial discipline. Many businesses in Nigeria operate primarily through WhatsApp, yet their transactions are often recorded informally — or not recorded at all.

We solve this by transforming simple WhatsApp messages into structured transaction records. Over time this creates clean Sales Registers, Expense Registers, Cashbooks, Customer Ledgers and other essential financial views — without forcing business owners to learn complicated accounting software.

Our Mission

To make the fundamental habit of daily transaction recording simple, sustainable and rewarding for Nigerian SMEs. We believe consistent record-keeping is the first and most important step toward business clarity, better decisions, and eventual access to formal finance.

What Makes Us Different

  • Habit-first design (streaks, gentle nudges, weekly feedback)
  • WhatsApp-native — no new app required
  • Focus on real registers businesses actually need
  • Calm, human, realistic approach (no hype)

How Receipta Ledgers Works

From a simple WhatsApp message to clean financial records.

1. Daily Logging

Send sales or expenses in plain language or a short structured format. Receipta understands natural messages and converts them into proper entries.

2. Automatic Registers

Every message updates your Sales Register, Expense Register, Cashbook and (where relevant) Customer or Supplier Ledger.

3. Habit Support

Streak counters, grace days, weekly summaries and milestone recognition help you stay consistent without feeling punished.

4. Clear Reports

Generate simple Profit & Loss views, Cash Flow summaries and other reports when you need them — all from the same underlying ledger.
