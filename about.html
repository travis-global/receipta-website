Building Financial Discipline for Everyday Businesses

Receipta exists to help micro, small, and informal businesses develop consistent financial discipline. Many businesses today operate primarily through messaging platforms like WhatsApp, but their transactions are often recorded informally or not recorded at all.

Receipta solves this problem by transforming simple WhatsApp messages into structured transaction records, helping businesses organize their daily sales activity and gradually build reliable financial histories.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make financial discipline simple and accessible for micro and small businesses. Instead of replacing accounting software, Receipta focuses on the most fundamental step in financial management: consistent daily transaction recording.

By helping businesses log and structure their transactions through simple messaging workflows, Receipta enables them to develop clean, organized financial records that support better decision-making, business stability, and long-term growth.

Preparing Businesses for Modern Financial Systems

Structured Transaction Records

Receipta helps businesses convert everyday sales activity into organized financial records, creating clarity around daily operations.

Financial Discipline

Consistent transaction logging builds the habit of financial discipline, which is essential for sustainable business growth.

Operational Clarity

Clear records help businesses understand their performance, track activity, and make more informed decisions.

Preparation for Financial Systems

By building structured transaction histories, businesses become better positioned to adopt accounting tools, work with accountants, or access future financial services.
